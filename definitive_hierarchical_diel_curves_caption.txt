Figure X. Predator-associated variation in diel activity of mountain gazelle demographic classes and the pooled population. Predicted normalized diel activity of juveniles (A), adult females (B), adult males (C), and pooled gazelles (D) across continuous gradients of site-level wolf and jackal activity. Low and high predator activity correspond to predictions at −1 and +1 SD, respectively, along the continuous wolf and jackal activity gradients; these values do not represent categorical camera groups. Curves are population-level predictions from hierarchical binomial cyclic generalized additive models and integrate to one within each plotted predator condition; shaded bands represent simulation-based 95% intervals. Statistical boxes summarize predator-by-time terms from the selected model and their robustness across prespecified sensitivity analyses. The selected pooled model retained wolf only, so panel D displays only the low- and high-wolf predictions. Apparent solar time aligns solar noon at 12:00 but does not standardize sunrise and sunset. Panels use independent y-axis scales to preserve class-specific curve readability.
